## What this page is for
Every change an administrator made, with the before and after state and a rollback where one is safe.

## Concepts
- **Entry** — who changed what, when, and from which address.
- **Snapshot** — the stored state a rollback restores.
- **Chain** — entries are hash-linked; a broken chain means the table was edited outside the console.

## First tasks
- Open an entry after an unexpected change to see exactly which field moved.
- Roll back a bad policy change instead of retyping it.
