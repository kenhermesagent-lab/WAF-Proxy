## What this page is for
Outside information about addresses and threats: feeds, lookups, source explorer, bundles and CrowdSec.

## Concepts
- **Feed** — a list of addresses from a third party, refreshed on a schedule.
- **Lookup** — the reputation and history of one address.
- **CrowdSec** — an optional decision source; the built-in WAF works without it.

## First tasks
- Look up an address you see in Logs to see what the feeds already know about it.
- Enable a feed only if you intend to act on it; every feed widens what gets blocked.
